# Oana Grigore

Lead Product Designer for complex web apps, with a brand and visual design background. Accessibility, design systems, product strategy.

I don't start designing until the problem is clear. Around 15 years in design, the last 8+ on complex web products: from brand work at Electronic Arts, to being the only designer across 40+ Shopify apps at Vitals, to leading design for five transportation brands, including a NEMT platform used in Virginia and Colorado. 

I ask a lot of questions, build the system before the screens, and treat accessibility as non-negotiable.

### What I've worked on

**Technology Oriented.** Lead Product Designer (2025–2026). Led product design across five transportation brands, including MediDrive, a non-emergency medical transportation platform for Virginia and Colorado. Flows where a confusing screen means a missed ride, not just a lost conversion.

**Vitals.** Senior Product Designer (2019–2025). Solo designer across 40+ Shopify apps. Built and governed the design system that made that possible, and worked on conversion and flow optimisation across the suite.

**Dillali.** Brand & Product Design Consultant (2021–2024).

### Methods I use often

| Area | What that means in practice |
|------|-----------------------------|
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.1 AA from the first wireframe, not as a final audit; accessible components baked into the system |
| Design systems & governance | Tokens, component libraries, documentation and the rules that keep a system alive across many products |
| Product strategy & discovery | Problem framing, scoping, competitor and differentiator research, turning ambiguity into a clear direction |
| Flow & conversion optimisation | Simplifying complex multi-step flows, reducing friction, measuring what changed |
| AI-assisted design | Brand and context set up first, research and problem framing with AI, multiple variants generated, refined in Figma, rules shared back with the team |
